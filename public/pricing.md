## Engagement Models

### Advisory
- Scope: Executive advisory, architecture review, second-opinion engagements.
- Duration: 2-12 weeks.
- Delivery: Principal-led, written deliverables, board-ready documents.
- Included: Diagnostic, written strategy, executive readouts. Excludes hands-on implementation.
- Commercial: Fixed-fee or monthly retainer. Contact for quote.

### Project
- Scope: Defined delivery engagements with fixed start, end, and acceptance criteria.
- Duration: 8-36 weeks.
- Delivery: Scoped team (principal + consultants + engineers), milestone-gated.
- Included: Discovery, architecture, implementation, acceptance testing, handover documentation, 30-day defect support.
- Commercial: Fixed-fee per phase or capped time-and-materials. Contact for quote.

### Retainer
- Scope: Continuous engagement across multiple workstreams or ongoing product development.
- Duration: 6-24 months, renewable.
- Delivery: Embedded team with quarterly business reviews.
- Included: Defined monthly capacity, priority access, roadmap integration, monthly reporting.
- Commercial: Monthly fee based on committed capacity. Contact for quote.

### Outcome-Indexed
- Scope: Engagements where commercial terms are tied to measurable business outcomes (ARR, cost reduction, conversion, compliance milestones).
- Duration: 6-18 months.
- Delivery: Shared-risk structure; Onsective compensation varies with defined KPI attainment.
- Included: Scoped team plus quarterly outcome measurement; contract includes agreed baseline and measurement methodology.
- Commercial: Base fee plus success component. Contact for structure.

## What Is Always Included

- Principal-led engagement — every project has a named senior Onsective principal accountable from start to close.
- Written deliverables — every engagement produces documents a client can supervise against.
- Handover and institutional capability — when we leave, the client team can operate what we built.
- Canadian data residency by default for engagements involving PIPEDA-scoped data.
- Regulatory fluency for regulated sectors (banking, healthcare, legal, government).

## What Is Never Included

- Offshore content sweatshops.
- Contingent or commission-only referral schemes.
- Engagements that bypass Law Society, provincial, or federal regulatory requirements.
- Work that cannot be disclosed to or audited by client counsel.
